BaginCamComp_*_feat.mlmodelc / BaginCamComp_*_head.mlmodelc

Model: GAIC - Grid Anchor based Image Cropping (Zeng, Li, Cao, Zhang; CVPR 2019 / TPAMI 2020)
Bobot terlatih: https://github.com/bo-zhang-cs/GAIC-Pytorch (implementasi tak resmi, MIT),
berdasarkan https://github.com/HuiZeng/Grid-Anchor-based-Image-Cropping-Pytorch (MIT).
Dikonversi ke Core ML oleh tools/comp_model/convert_gaic.py (fp16). Backbone: MobileNetV2, ShuffleNetV2, VGG16.
